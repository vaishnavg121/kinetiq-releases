KINETIQ bundles a normalized subset of SR Legacy April 2018 and FNDDS 2021–2023,
published 31 October 2024. Processed 5 November 2026. Original source IDs, food
descriptions, portion weights, nutrient units and nullable missing values are retained.
See catalog/foods-manifest.json for counts and archive checksums. This is composition
data, not a measurement of an individual recipe or a guarantee of current branded labels.
